03 / Prototypes

Two-week mechanic prototypes

Alongside live games I prototype hybrid-casual concepts quickly. I break down competitor levels, write a rule spec, then build a level editor, solver-verified levels and greedy and random playtest bots. Each concept takes about two weeks, so the team can judge it on evidence rather than a pitch.

This one is a picture shooter: colour banks clear a pixel picture, and the order you fire them in decides whether you jam. Other prototypes are unreleased, and I'm happy to walk through them in an interview.
